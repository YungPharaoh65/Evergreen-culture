ABOUT PROJECT:

The Evergreen project composes of 3 sections of the entire project 
that is implemented in the creation of the projects:

The client site - what the client sees when they log into their site
The admin dashboard - details of the logged in users as well as their orders and services
The Service Providor - The people advertising their services to the clients who want it done proffesionally   

This site ensures that all parties of the project are satidsfied 
with what it is that they are provided with in terms of service delivery 
and the regard of satisfying both customer and provider

This site is a host for all three areas of business
 B2B
 B2C
 C2C

 it covers up as well the aspects of giving serviceas well as receiving 
 it. The puropse of the Evergreen project is to benefit
 from it by having nature as part of your surrounding, whether
 you are an experienced gardener / farmer looking to explore
 other options of plantation, to a person showing interest 
 in gardening wanting to learn how. 
 Or even having someone have them set it up for them. 

 The project enables users multiple options at a time eusuring that
 they have a better chance at choosing what they want and need for
 a perfect garden experience. 
 
 The project also provides a platform for users to be able to 
 state their views about the company that they have around
 showing transparency between both parties.
